About Me

I operate at the nexus of artisanal mastery and tactical risk mitigation. My career has not followed a traditional corporate path, but has instead been a calculated evolution from high-stakes field operations to the management of global infrastructure. Whether I am perfecting the viscosity of a record-breaking teh tarik pull or auditing the systemic failure points of a financial institution, my mandate remains the same: absolute precision.

My career began in the non-permissive environments of low-signature asset recovery with Diamond Dogs. It was here that I mastered "On-Site Procurement" (OSP), learning to execute complex missions with minimal external support or equipment. This phase of my life proved that success is not a result of brute force, but of technical precision and environmental adaptation—lessons I now apply to the rigorous standards of the University of Brewing.

Transitioning into the urban sector with CrimeNet, I shifted my focus to institutional vulnerability auditing. By leading multi-disciplinary "Red Team" crews through simulated breach scenarios for Tier-1 entities, I gained a deep understanding of the technical lifecycle of security, from signal jamming to hard-site extraction. This era honed my ability to manage high-pressure personnel and technical systems, a skill set that now ensures the operational integrity of my beverage ventures.

Today, as a Principal Partner at SecuroServ and the Co-Founder of Tealive, I manage the delicate balance between public-facing hospitality and subterranean logistical networks. I direct the overarching strategy for a diversified portfolio of luxury hospitality and logistical infrastructures, ensuring absolute administrative anonymity and liquidity for high-net-worth interests. I utilize an operative’s eye for detail to drive an executive’s vision of growth.

While I am a recognized coffee connoisseur and an expert boba tea selector, I maintain the same discipline in the kitchen as I did in the field. I build environments where interests can flourish untouched by external volatility—though I maintain one strict operational boundary: please don't beg for free Tealive.
